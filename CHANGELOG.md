## Unreleased

- 2026-11-05: CAD audit. `mechanical/cad/cad_audit.py` measures every STEP and DXF the model writes and compares them with 269 values taken from the design rules and the UCI spec, plus 378 generic checks (one solid, valid, closed, every loop classified, no cut overlaps another, DXF matches its STEP section loop by loop, drum parts unwrap onto their flat patterns, every M6 hole on a rail, assembly re-checked for overlap). `fusion_audit.py` repeated the measurements inside Autodesk Fusion for an independent kernel (V-014): 105 bodies, 423 comparisons, no difference beyond 2 ppm in volume and none in position. Found and fixed: console face sloping the wrong way, bay ceiling plate in three pieces, cord hole through an M6 hole, louvers through the exhaust port, panel holes where no rail is, belt track 77 mm off the pulley, rollers overhanging the drum. Model draft 3: drum sheet now carries its tabs and every hole, rings and splice carry their holes, baffles are 40 x 40 x 3 angles with holes, front and rear panels split at 260, DXFs in world coordinates on a CUT layer. Design rules draft 4.
