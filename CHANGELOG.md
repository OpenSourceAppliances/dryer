## Unreleased

- 2026-11-06: Model draft 5, the realism pass. Everything the design rules called for is now drawn: T-slot profiles on all 25 extrusions (8 mm slot 4040, 6 mm slot 2020, centre bores), 6 mm laser corner radii on every plate (3 on the cheeks, splice and tabs), every panel bolt (M6 x 12 hex with washer, 175 of them, each sitting in a slot), the four leveling feet in the posts' tapped bores through 8.5 holes in the base pan, the belt drawn on its real path with the flat idler, its arm, pivot and spring brackets and spring, the door's eight M4 ring bolts, two flag hinges, D-handle, seal and magnetic catch, the Tier 0 module (faceplate with engraved legends, two timers with yokes and knobs, selector, START, K1 on its DIN rail), the rear felt seal, the cord bushing and cord, and detail on the motor (end bells, shafts, terminal box, two cradle mounts), pulley (grooves, hub) and blower (inlet mouth, outlet box into the exhaust duct). 110 parts, no overlaps. Drawing the belt showed that the single ceiling slot on the centre line could not pass it (the runs cross the plate at X -138 and +148); the bay ceiling now has two slots. Drawing the felt showed the lint slot in the felt annulus, outside the ring bore and with its ends outside the drum (V-016 fails on paper; options listed, decision open). Drawing the Tier 0 module showed the 120 mm UCI envelope does not fit inside the console wedge (V-015, open). Drum bolts M4 x 8 (not 10) and M6 x 12 at the baffles (the holes were always 6.5). The audit grew to 320 document checks and 404 generic checks (rounded outlines, bulge arcs in DXF polylines, the faceplate's own drawing matched to its STEP section); all pass. Fusion: `fusion_motion.py` draft 2 joints the real knobs, hinge, idler and door hardware (14 joints), applies library appearances to every component, and its interference analysis at three poses is clean at rest; only the heat module tray's path through the drive remains (V-021, decision open). Archive with joints and appearances in `mechanical/fusion/`. Design rules draft 5, BOM lines for the hinges, handle, hardware, idler arm and motor mounts.
