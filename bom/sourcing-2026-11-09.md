# Sourcing pass, 2026-11-09

Carts were filled at seven sellers for one electric reference build (Tier 0). Nothing was ordered. Prices are the sellers' listed prices on 2026-11-09, before shipping and tax, and will drift. This file records what went into each cart, what was substituted, and what was left out on purpose.

Read `docs/verification.md` before ordering. Gate 1 measures bought parts against the drawings, and several drawings wait on those measurements. Buying the parts below is how Gate 1 starts. Cutting plates is not part of this pass.

## Not carted, and why

## Drive and blower (decided later on 2026-11-09)

The drawn drive cannot be bought: no catalog 1/3 hp 1725 rpm 48-frame motor has a shaft on each end, and the smallest catalog J sheave on a 1/2 in bore would spin the drum at about twice the target speed. An appliance dryer motor (Whirlpool 279827) solves the speed with ribs ground into its shaft, but was rejected because it ties the machine to one maker. The drive is now a stock motor through a countershaft, and the blower has its own motor (`DECISIONS.md` 2026-11-09). The model will be redrawn to it in draft 10.
