Read `docs/verification.md` before ordering. Gate 1 measures bought parts against the drawings, and several drawings wait on those measurements. Buying the parts below is how Gate 1 starts. Cutting plates is not part of this pass.

## Not carted, and why

- Drum motor, motor pulley, drum belt and idler. Grainger has no 1/3 hp, 1725 rpm, dual-shaft NEMA 48 motor with two 1/2 in shafts; its matches are 3450 rpm, 1075 or 1625 rpm, or HVAC replacements. McMaster's J-section belts stop at 55 in and its smallest J pulley is 2.5 in OD, against the drawn 5-rib belt of about 2232 mm and a 20 mm pitch motor pulley. The drive needs a decision first: either find a catalog motor and a source for the small pulley and the long belt, or move to the appliance-market dryer motor, belt and idler family, which would change the drive drawings and needs a `DECISIONS.md` entry.
- Blower wheel and housing. Its bore and rotation follow the motor, so it waits on the same decision.
- Laser-cut plates (steel, stainless, aluminium). `docs/verification.md` marks several DXFs as not to be cut until their Gate 1 checks close, and those checks need the parts above in hand. Upload the DXFs to a cutting service after the checks, not before.
- Strain relief. Size it to the cord that arrives (V-112).
- Wire, twisted pair, ferrules, ring terminals, quick-disconnects and wire labels. The BOM sets their quantities when the wiring box is drawn.
- Mini-Fit Jr. and JST XH crimp tools.
- Door hinges, door handle, magnetic catch, lint mesh and pull, door seal, drum felt, glide stock, silicone foam gasket, brass barb and silicone tube, hose clamps, 4 in duct pipe, 5 in starting collar, ground stud kit, paint. These are common hardware; match each to its drawing before buying.
- The 120 V cord for the gas, heat pump and no-heat builds. Not needed for the electric build.
